"We already use…"

Keep what works. Boris fills the gaps between your tools.
"We already use Eve."
Eve is strong on case work. Ask who runs your marketing numbers: cost per signed case, reviews, ad alerts. Boris does that from the same brain that answered the call. And with Boris, nothing reaches a client until a person taps Approve.
"We use Smith.ai, Ruby or LEX for phones."
An answering service takes a message, and then your team still works the lead. Boris answers, scores the case, writes it into your case software and has the retainer ready for your OK, in the same minute. Keep your service for overflow, and start Boris on nights and weekends.
"We use EvenUp or Supio for demands."
Keep it. Boris is the rest of the firm: phones, intake, unsigned-retainer chases, client updates and marketing. His Paralegal desk is there when you want one brain from first call to settlement.
"We use Clio Grow or Lawmatics for intake."
That's intake inside one CRM. Boris covers the phone call before it and the client updates and ad numbers after it. He writes into the software you already run. No fee per converted lead, and never a cut of a case.
"We use Hona for client updates."
Boris does "where's my case?" too, and he remembers what the client said on their first call, because it's the same brain that took it. No hand-offs, and clients don't get asked the same questions twice.
"Is our data safe?"
Each firm gets its own isolated server. Your data is never used for training. You can see everything Boris did in an audit log, and you choose what he can read, draft or act on for every connection.
