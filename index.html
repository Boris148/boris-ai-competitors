A closer look: Eve

Eve is the closest match. Here's an honest side-by-side.

Eve (EveOS) is a well-funded AI platform built for plaintiff firms. Its strength is the paralegal and attorney work product: chronologies, demands, discovery, motions and research.

~$1B valuation (Sept 2025) ~$164M raised · a16z, Spark, Lightspeed, Menlo 1,200–1,700+ plaintiff firms (Eve's own figures vary) PI · employment · med mal · disability · workers' comp
Where Eve is strong
  • Attorney work product: medical chronologies, demand letters, discovery (propounding and responding), motions, complaints and built-in legal research.
  • Intake agent: answers calls 24/7 in 28 languages, scores leads and can send the engagement letter before the call ends.
  • Auditor: reviews every active matter each night for missed injuries, treatment gaps and the next step. Analyst answers firm questions in plain English (in beta).
  • Trust: says it's SOC 2 Type 2 and HIPAA compliant, keeps data isolated per firm and doesn't train shared models on it.
  • Named results: case studies from firms like Mike Morse Law Firm and Frontier Law Center.
  • Pricing: not published. Sold through a demo with the sales team.
Where Boris is different
  • Public prices, buy online. Every job has a flat monthly price on hireboris.com, and a firm can hire Boris online without a sales call. Never a cut of a case.
  • The whole front office: phones, intake, follow-ups until the retainer is signed, Case Manager, Marketing (where cases come from and what each signed case cost) and an Ads Manager for Meta, Google and Local Services ads.
  • One named employee. Boris is one AI employee with one Firm Brain, and your team texts or chats with him in plain English. He isn't a set of separate tools.
  • You set the leash: Read, Draft or Act for each connection. He asks first before money, new ads or anything sent in your firm's name.
Short version: if your bottleneck is drafting and discovery, look hard at Eve. If it's the front office (calls, sign-ups, client check-ins and knowing what your ads really cost), that's Boris. Many firms will want both.

"We already use…"

Keep what works. Boris fills the gaps between your tools.
"We already use Eve."
Keep it. Eve is strong on the attorney work product. Boris runs the front office around it: phones, sign-ups, client check-ins, and marketing and ads (cost per signed case, reviews, ad alerts), all from the same brain that answered the call. See the side-by-side.
"We use Smith.ai, Ruby or LEX for phones."
An answering service takes a message, and then your team still works the lead. Boris answers, scores the case, writes it into your case software and has the retainer ready for your OK, in the same minute. Keep your service for overflow, and start Boris on nights and weekends.
"We use EvenUp or Supio for demands."
Keep it. Boris is the rest of the firm: phones, intake, unsigned-retainer chases, client updates and marketing. His Paralegal desk is there when you want one brain from first call to settlement.
"We use Clio Grow or Lawmatics for intake."
That's intake inside one CRM. Boris covers the phone call before it and the client updates and ad numbers after it. He writes into the software you already run. No fee per converted lead, and never a cut of a case.
"We use Hona for client updates."
Boris does "where's my case?" too, and he remembers what the client said on their first call, because it's the same brain that took it. No hand-offs, and clients don't get asked the same questions twice.
"Is our data safe?"
Each firm gets its own isolated server. Your data is never used for training. You can see everything Boris did in an audit log, and you choose what he can read, draft or act on for every connection.
